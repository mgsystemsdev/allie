Bird prey — what's applicable for Allie
Can Allie eat birds?
At 9 months, day-old chicks are the only bird-type prey appropriate for Allie, and only as an occasional variety item — not a staple. Quail becomes an option at sub-adult stage (~1.5 kg+). Pigeon/pigeon-sized birds are for large adults only. Birds are high fat, so use sparingly even then.

Sizing rule
Prey should be no wider than 1.0–1.5× the widest point of Allie's body. A slight visible lump after feeding is perfect. A large bulge = size down. No lump at all = size up.
